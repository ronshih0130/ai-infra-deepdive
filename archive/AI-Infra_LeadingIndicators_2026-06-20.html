Highest-alpha contradictions

Per the framework, the best signals come from contradictions & bottleneck rotation (capex rising but cloud prices falling; backlog rising but margins falling).

  • BACKLOG UP, EQUITY DOWN (CLOUD): capex and RPO/backlog are surging (ORCL RPO $638B +363%, 97.5% GPU utilization) WHILE the stocks sold off on the same prints (ORCL -7/-10%, CRWV -59% off highs) — the market is repricing financing/leverage/depreciation, not demand. Inter-AI-name correlation fell 80%->20%: the tape is now discriminating WHO monetizes the capex, not WHETHER demand exists. Highest-alpha because it separates real demand from balance-sheet risk.
  • CAPEX UP, RENTS DOWN (ACCEL/MARGIN): hyperscaler capex rising to ~$725B 2026 with new-gen GPU/XPU sold out, YET prior-gen H100/H200 cloud rents fell ~64-85% — vintage depreciation, not demand softness, but it caps pricing power on older SKUs and is the late-cycle obsolescence watch-item.
  • BACKLOG UP, MARGINS DOWN (SERVER/MARGIN): record backlogs (DELL $51.3B, VRT $15B) WHILE box-builder gross margins bifurcate/collapse (SMCI ~6-10%, ODM-direct disintermediation) — proves the value is in scarce upstream content (power/optics) and customer lock-in, NOT box assembly. SMCI is the avoid/short-side expression; DELL/CLS the quality longs.
  • SHORTAGE REAL, EQUITY PRICED (PASSIVE/POWER pure-plays): the MLCC and power-silicon shortages are genuinely underpriced in the fundamentals but the pure-plays are NOT (Murata +169%, NVTS +262%, VICR +145% YTD) — so the alpha sits in the diluted-discount large-caps (VSH, IFX, ON) where the risk is execution/mix, not the cycle.
  • VSH ~4,000x P/E is a trailing-earnings artifact (just crossed back to profit), NOT evidence MLCC pricing is over-extended — forward multiple is far lower; the diversified names carry execution/dilution risk, not pure cycle risk.
  • MEM all-aligned with no clean contradiction (capex/prices/margins/backlog all up, sold out) — the honest caution is the FLIP SIDE: a commodity at peak; every prior memory supercycle ended in oversupply, so the only risk is timing the roll, watched via spot prices + 2027 wafer/HBM adds.
