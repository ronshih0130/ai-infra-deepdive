Executive snapshot

為什麼是現在

AI clusters are scaling toward millions of GPUs. Copper is out of reach, so every added GPU now needs light to talk to its neighbours — and the bottleneck has migrated all the way down to the single hardest input: the indium-phosphide (InP) EML laser chip. Pricing power concentrates where capacity is scarcest.

EXPLAIN IT SIMPLY · 白話版

Think of an AI datacenter as a city where the GPUs are buildings that must constantly talk to each other. Copper wires are like short footpaths — fine next door, useless across town — so the city runs on light (optical links). To make light you need a tiny, very hard-to-build laser chip, and those lasers are grown on a rare crystal wafer called indium phosphide (InP). Right now the world can't make enough of these laser chips — that shortage is the chokepoint, and whoever controls it earns outsized profits. The investing question this report answers: which companies own the chokepoint, and is the shortage still a secret (early) or already in the share price (late)?

03

Layer 3 — the light sources, illustrated

光源元件

The four ways to make light for an AI link. Three of the four still depend on indium phosphide — which is exactly why the bottleneck is structural, not a one-quarter blip.

200G EML CHOKEPOINT

Electro-absorption modulated laser — laser and modulator integrated on one InP die. The default source for 800G/1.6T pluggables; modulates light directly, no external modulator needed.

Vol. supply: Lumentum (only 200G), Coherent, DSBJ

CW + SiPh SCALING

A continuous-wave InP laser feeds a silicon-photonics chip that does the modulating. The main alternative route at 1.6T+ — but the CW laser is still InP, so it doesn't escape the upstream pinch.

PIC foundry: TSMC COUPE, GlobalFoundries, X-FAB, Tower

External Laser (ELS) CPO ENABLER

For CPO, the laser is pulled out of the hot switch/GPU package and mounted externally (better reliability, serviceability), feeding the co-packaged optics by fiber. Still InP-based.

Sources: Lumentum, Coherent, AAOI pump lasers

TFLN modulator EMERGING

Thin-film lithium-niobate Mach-Zehnder modulator — very high bandwidth/low loss, a candidate for 400G/lane and beyond. Modulator only; still needs a CW laser to supply the light.

Early-stage PIC integration; pairs with InP/SiPh

08

The chokepoint — recent months

近月卡點

200G-per-lane InP EML laser chips

The single component gating 1.6T transceiver delivery, built on indium phosphide with brutal 15–50% yields and multi-year capacity lead times. Lumentum is the only supplier shipping 200G EMLs at volume. Nvidia's ~$4B lock-up reserved essentially all capacity through CY2027 under long-term agreements; anyone needing more than their LTA pays premium spot. Analysts expect double-digit price increases on 200G EMLs in 2026 with no qualified second source — and because both EML and the CW-laser alternative use InP, the constraint can't be designed around quickly.

How to be early next time 如何提前布局

1 · Watch the physical, not the headline

Lead-times, spot pricing, book-to-bill and capex guides move weeks-to-quarters before earnings. That gap is the alpha window — the CPU/MLCC playbook.

2 · Go one layer upstream

When a small substrate / materials name starts winning design-win PRs while still cheap and under-covered, that's the early entry — before sell-side notices.

3 · Sell the recognition, not the news

Once the stock has tripled and upgrades flood in, the asymmetry is gone. AXTI (+440%) and LITE (+141%) are past that point — own them for compounding, not a re-rating.

4 · The neutral arms-dealer is underrated

A name that wins regardless of which vendor prevails (packaging, test, substrate) carries less single-bet risk and often re-rates later — that's where remaining asymmetry sits today.
