EXPLAIN IT SIMPLY · 白話版

This dashboard answers four questions in order. (1) What's the weather? rates/growth/inflation set whether the tide lifts boats. It got worse this week on the macro axis: growth is cooling fast and inflation is still hot (a "stagflation-lite" mix), and now the Fed looks more likely to raise than cut this year — that caps how expensive stocks can get. (2) Is the tank near empty? — how greedy/leveraged the crowd is. Two weeks ago this was flashing red (record leverage, everyone all-in). This week a lot of that unwound: fund managers cut exposure, retail bulls capitulated, and stretched prices came back toward their averages. That's healthier, not scarier — a washout that's already bouncing. The one thing still stuck at an extreme is borrowed money (margin debt), but that's a slow, lagging gauge. (3) Which warning lights actually work? — we backtest each so we don't act on ones that merely feel predictive (the yield curve and component-shortage signals genuinely lead; "the market looks overheated" by itself does not reliably time tops). (4) Where is money trending vs snapping back? — at the sector level and inside AI-infra: a momentum sub-segment you ride; a mean-reversion one you buy on dips, not chase. This week the memory and optics names got hit while networking and power held — so the dip-buy list is where fundamentals stayed strong but price pulled back.

Mechanical 2-of-3 hedge rule

Hedge (raise cash / add downside) only when ≥2 of 3 leading lights flip red: (1) curve re-inverts (10Y–3M < 0), (2) HY-OAS breaks >350bp, (3) breadth <50% >200DMA. Status: 0 of 3 red (curve +61, HY ~275, breadth ~58%). The record-leverage reading and the Fed hike-tail are context — they raise the cost of being wrong, so they argue for trimming size and tightening stops, not for triggering the hedge by themselves. Honesty note: this 2-of-3 rule is a discipline overlay, not a backtested edge — each light leads in isolation, but the combined rule has too few historical firings on this 2-yr window to call it proven; it is a pre-committed response plan, not a forecast — the value is discipline (a decided-in-advance reaction), not prediction. The directional evidence each light leads is well-documented (curve inversion → recession ~6–12mo; HY-OAS breakout → equity stress; breadth <50% → index tops), which is why the composite is a reasonable response trigger even without a proven combined-rule edge on this short window.

De-risking waterfall (if lights flip)

  • 1 light red: stop adding new money; let winners run with trailing stops.
  • 2 lights red: trim the most-extended momentum (Networking-Si, MU); raise 10–20% cash.
  • 3 lights red: cut high-beta AI to core; hedge index; hold only cheap/defensive anchors (NVDA, ETN, XLV).
  • Hawkish-Fed override: if Jul-29 delivers a hike, pre-emptively trim the richest multiples (GEV/ALAB/MRVL) even at 0-of-3.
  • Re-risk: when ≥2 lights are green AND price reclaims the 20w MA.
