Where the leading data is — and which sub-segment has alpha

Data-acquisition map for catching AI-infra shortages / content-value shifts before they print. As-of 2026-06-19. Personal research — NOT investment advice. IBKR read-only.

The game is the product cycle: a new platform (Rubin now, Feynman next) raises some component's content-value per board while capacity expands on a slow physical clock. The alpha is to spot that node — and the under-covered vendor exposed to it — while it's still latent. You can only do that where you actually hold leading data. This map shows, per sub-segment, which of the 8 signal layers a retail investor can source as truly-leading (free), leading (paid/effort), confirming, or blind — then ranks sub-segments by alpha-catchability (leading-data richness × how under-covered the names are).

3 · Alpha-catchability ranking

Leading-data richness × under-coverage factor, indexed to 100. High = you can see the signal early and the names aren't fully priced. Low = either blind, or priced the same day (no retail edge).

The data-rich, under-covered Taiwan/Japan/Korea supply-chain nodes (passives, power, cooling, substrate, optics) are where a retail investor can realistically be early. The financialized mega-cap nodes (compute silicon, HBM, demand) have data but no edge — covered names reprice the same afternoon any tell appears.

6 · Trigger logic (fire on content-driven shortage, not capex beta)

A sub-segment fires when ≥4 of 6 agree, with steps 1 & 2 mandatory:

  1. 1. Content-UP (L1) — next-platform teardown/OCP shows $/board or units/board ↑ GoG [mandatory]
  2. 2. Capacity-SLOW (L2) — supplier capacity timeline >12mo out [mandatory]
  3. 3. Design-win named (L1/L7) — bill-of-lading + DigiTimes/法說會 name the exposed (ideally under-covered) vendor
  4. 4. Tightness confirming (L3) — basket lead-time stair-steps + stock drains across ≥2 distis, OR utilization >90%
  5. 5. Input/price pre-stage (L4) — raw-input/FX move or adjacent-passive price rise (corroborator)
  6. 6. Hard confirm (L6) — the named vendor's monthly revenue (MOPS) inflects up

Then Engine-C gates: financialization veto (>15 analysts + deep options ⇒ not EARLY, route to under-covered exposure) · reverse-model priced-test (enter only if modeled content jump > what price discounts) · cross-read on the same-quarter cross-section, never the pooled time-series. Thresholds above are uncalibrated starting points — run the negative-control/base-rate pass before trading them.
