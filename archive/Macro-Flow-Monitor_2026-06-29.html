EXPLAIN IT SIMPLY · 白話版

This dashboard answers four questions in order. (1) What's the weather? rates/growth/inflation set whether the tide lifts boats. It just got worse on both axes: growth is cooling and inflation is re-heating (a mild "stagflation" mix), so the Fed can't cut and can't get friendlier. (2) Is the tank near empty? — how greedy/leveraged the crowd is. Borrowed money to buy stocks (margin debt) just hit an all-time record and a famous Wall-Street froth gauge (BofA's) flashed a rare sell; yet the public's mood is fearful. That combination — fearful talk, maxed-out leveraged positions — is historically the riskier one. (3) Which of our warning lights actually work? — we backtest each so we don't act on ones that merely feel predictive (the yield curve and component-shortage signals genuinely lead; "the market looks overheated" by itself does not reliably time tops). (4) Where is money trending vs snapping back? — at the sector level and inside AI-infra: a momentum sub-segment you ride; a mean-reversion one you buy on dips, not chase. Right now most of AI-infra has already pulled back, which is where the dip-buy list comes from.

BACKTEST CHECK — does "overheat" reliably mean "sell"?

Mechanical 2-of-3 hedge rule

Hedge (raise cash / add downside) only when ≥2 of 3 leading lights flip red: (1) curve re-inverts (10Y–3M < 0), (2) HY-OAS breaks >350bp, (3) breadth <50% >200DMA. Status: 0 of 3 red (curve +62, HY ~280, breadth ~60). The record-leverage / BofA-sell readings are context — they raise the cost of being wrong, so they argue for trimming size and tightening stops, not for triggering the hedge by themselves. Honesty note: this 2-of-3 rule is a discipline overlay, not a backtested edge — each light leads in isolation, but the combined rule has too few historical firings on this 2-yr window to call it proven; it is a pre-committed response plan, not a forecast.

De-risking waterfall (if lights flip)

  • 1 light red: stop adding new money; let winners run with trailing stops.
  • 2 lights red: trim the most-extended momentum (Networking-Si, MU); raise 10–20% cash.
  • 3 lights red: cut high-beta AI to core; hedge index; hold only cheap/defensive anchors (NVDA, ETN, XLV).
  • Re-risk: when ≥2 lights flip back green AND price reclaims the 20w MA.

L3 taxonomy — what each bucket includes / excludes (kept MECE)

Compute fabric = foundry + memory + the front-end packaging tools that make the die-stack (TSM, MU, AMAT); excludes the OSAT/substrate that follows. · Substrates / adv packaging = OSAT assembly, ABF/glass substrate, packaging metrology/inspection (AMKR, KLAC); the line vs compute-fabric is front-end (fabric) vs back-end assembly (substrate). · Networking silicon = SerDes / retimer / AEC / switch-&-custom ASIC silicon (AVGO, MRVL, CRDO, ALAB); ANET (switch systems) is held as a reference, not in the basket. · Optics = transceivers, EML/CW lasers, the optical interconnect (COHR, LITE, FN). · Cooling = thermal: cold-plate, CDU, liquid loop (VRT, MOD). · Power delivery = grid→rack: transformers, switchgear, VRM/48V (ETN, GEV, MPWR). KLAC sits in Substrates as the process-control/inspection that gates back-end yield; VRT is counted once, in Cooling. Cooling & Power converge at the rack but are split by function (heat-out vs power-in) to avoid double-counting; NVDA is the demand anchor, in no basket.
