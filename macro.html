Read this first — in plain language

What happened. A war premium went into oil, and oil went into the interest-rate market. Because energy feeds inflation, traders suddenly decided the Fed might raise rates on 29 July rather than sit still — the odds of a hike went from about 1-in-10 to better than 1-in-3 in ten days. Higher expected rates hurt assets priced on far-away profits, which is exactly what AI-infrastructure stocks are. So money moved out of Technology and Semiconductors and into Financials, Industrials, Energy and Utilities.

The strange part. Ordinary investors turned very pessimistic — the AAII survey has not been this bearish since September 2022. Normally that is a contrarian buy signal. But those same investors are carrying a record $1.5 trillion of borrowed money in their brokerage accounts. They said they were scared; they did not sell. That gap is the most important thing on this page, because a market that is frightened but still leveraged is a market that can still be forced to sell.

What we did about it. Nothing. Every idea below is labelled HYPOTHESIS or WATCH — research states, not instructions. One watchlist row was demoted this week for failing the system's own two-family rule, the other is on notice, and the third slot was deliberately left empty — so the watchlist currently holds no watch-grade idea at all. This is a personal research note, not investment advice.

Glossary

RRG / quadrant — Relative Rotation Graph. Plots a security's relative strength against a benchmark (horizontal) versus the momentum of that relative strength (vertical). It rotates clockwise: Improving → Leading → Weakening → Lagging.
Hook — the tail has bent more than 90°, meaning the trajectory reversed. A warning that the quadrant reading is unstable.
Variance ratio / Hurst exponent — measures of whether a series trends or mean-reverts. Above 1 and above 0.5 respectively, moves tend to extend; below, they tend to reverse.
OAS (option-adjusted spread) — the extra yield high-yield bonds pay over Treasuries. Widening signals credit stress. 277bp is historically very tight.
Real yield — the Treasury yield after expected inflation, measured by TIPS. It is the opportunity cost of holding gold and the discount rate applied to far-off profits.
Effective n — the honest sample size after accounting for overlapping windows. Weekly data with an eight-week forward window carries roughly one-eighth the independent information the raw count implies.
Money dial — a coarse −2..+2 summary of whether system liquidity is expanding or contracting. Fitted on 2020–22 and known to have failed in 2023–24; a framing device only.
Signal family — an independent category of evidence (price, breadth, revisions, flows, cross-asset, fundamental). All price-derived signals count as ONE family, however many indicators they span.
LATENT / EARLY / RECOGNITION / PRICED — awareness stages. Only LATENT and EARLY carry discovery value; RECOGNITION and PRICED mean the market already knows.
Fail-closed — when a required input is missing, the system downgrades the conclusion rather than guessing. Missing data returns UNKNOWN, never a supportive default.
