This dashboard zooms out from individual chokepoints to ask three questions in order. (1) What's the weather? — interest rates, growth, inflation set whether the tide lifts all boats. THE DIVERGENCE (and the contrarian read)

Surveys (F&G 34, AAII bears at a 1-year high of 52%) say capitulation/fear — historically a contrarian-bullish setup for the next few weeks. But margin debt is at an all-time record ($1.30T, +6.8% m/m, 4.1% of GDP) and cash levels are low — i.e. the crowd is fearful in words yet maximally invested in deeds. That combination cuts both ways: a fear-driven dip is likely buyable (sentiment washout), but there is little dry powder and a lot of leverage, so an inflation/policy shock unwinds violently. Net: own the trend, but size for a leverage air-pocket.

Translation: a short-term risk-off / defensive rotation is happening inside a Tech-led bull — exactly what you'd expect with Fear sentiment and an energy-inflation scare, but an intact trend. Energy's leadership is now weakening (1m RS −9.6) even as the oil-price story dominates headlines — a classic "buy the rumour, the move is in the stocks" tell.

Rotation map & regime

輪動象限與型態

The same data as a rotation clock. RS-Ratio (x) = strength vs the index; RS-Momentum (y) = whether that strength is rising or falling. Sectors rotate clockwise: Improving → Leading → Weakening → Lagging.

Leading

Tech. Strong RS + rising — the durable momentum trend (trend R²=0.52, VR>1); own it, mind the 1-week cooldown.

Weakening

Lagging

Utilities, Staples, RE, Disc., Comm. Out of favour; defensives only bouncing on this week's fear.

Improving

Financials, Health Care. Deep laggards turning up from oversold — mean-reversion candidates, not trends yet (XLF VR=0.69).

Regime method (v2): for the heaviest names we compute the rigorous test from IBKR daily history — trend R² of log-price, lag-1 return autocorrelation, and the 5-day variance ratio (VR>1 = trending/momentum, VR<1 = mean-reverting). Confirmed: XLK R²=0.52, slope +56%/yr, VR=1.10 → Momentum; XLE R²=0.68 but VR=0.98 & 1m RS rolling over → Weakening/mean-rev; XLF R²=0.31, slope −15%/yr, AC1 −0.09, VR=0.69 → Improving/mean-rev.
