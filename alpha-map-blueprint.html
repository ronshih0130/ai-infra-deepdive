The Expectations Layer — Consensus-Gap & Nowcast Alpha

As-of 2026-06-16 · Codex gpt-5.5/xhigh reviewed (2 rounds) · personal research, NOT investment advice · IBKR READ-ONLY

The two existing engines sense tightness (chokepoint deep-dive) and regime + tape (macro monitor) — but never expectations. This layer makes the gap between your bottom-up estimate and Street consensus, and the second derivative of that gap, into first-class dated objects. The alpha is the window between the gap opening and the revision completing.

1The alpha mechanism — and where the retail edge actually is

You position inside one causal chain. For a mega-cap, steps [B] and [C] collapse to a single afternoon (the print hits every desk and algo at once) → no edge. Edge survives in only two places.

MODE 1 · CONSENSUS-GAP

Where a real Street number exists (REPORTED or validated RECONSTRUCTED consensus). Be early to a forced revision. EARLY = GAP_z>2 · completed workbook · reverse-margin>σ · dated catalyst · revision_progress<0.33.

MODE 2 · DISCOVERY / PRICE-IMPLIED

For under-covered names with NO_CONS — exactly where the alpha lives. No fake consensus. Anchor on the price-implied expectation; EARLY = MINE beats it by >σ in the conservative case · dated catalyst · tradeable expression. Plus the identifiability gate: SOTP-isolate the AI sliver · prove the driver isn’t already in price · catalyst must reveal that exact driver — else research-only.

The hard gate (mode-aware): miss any required gate → at most a variant baseline, logged CONTEXT, never funded. This two-mode split resolves the framework's deepest tension — the best alpha terrain (under-covered) is exactly where free consensus is weakest. Default verdict for most mega-caps in mid-2026: “real but already priced — no edge.” The most valuable output is sometimes a veto.
