1 · Price & awareness — a meme spike on a deep-cyclical base

Five-year monthly close (€). The asymmetric trade was the 2024–25 down-cycle into the €3.73 trough; the +288% trough-to-peak that followed ended in a 27-May-2026 meme spike to €15.88 on zero corporate news, now round-tripped to €9.19. Awareness stage: euphoria / sentiment-priced — not latent, not early.

2 · The 8-point teardown (8 點拆解)

① 怎麼賺錢
Pure-play specialty foundry — owns no chip designs; fabless/IDM customers send designs, X-FAB fabricates wafers on mature/specialty nodes (350–180nm CMOS, plus SiC/GaN, MEMS, BiCMOS, SOI, photonics) and bills per-wafer + NRE/prototyping. Value is in analog process recipes, not transistor density. (IFRS-15 over-time revenue distorts the headline margin.)
② 營收結構
FY2025 revenue $870.3M (+7% YoY). Core markets (auto+industrial+medical) = $814M = 94%. By tech (Q1'26): CMOS ~80%, Microsystems & Photonics ~17%, Wide-bandgap SiC/GaN ~8%. 6 fabs: Germany (Erfurt/Dresden/Itzehoe), France (Corbeil), Malaysia (Kuching/Sarawak — the newest fab, a CMOS/analog capacity ramp), US (Lubbock — X-FAB's 6-inch SiC line).
③ 成長來源
Two-speed. SECULAR-UP: Microsystems & Photonics +42% YoY to an ATH $33.7M (piezo-MEMS/AlScN, MEMS, ultrasonic, silicon-photonics). RECOVERING: SiC/WBG +152% YoY (off a crushed base; wafers +195%). DOWN: CMOS −9% YoY (auto destock). + ~€128M EU + ~$50M US Chips-Act capacity support.
④ 終端市場
Heavily AUTO-skewed (~62%) — the current drag (−10% YoY, Melexis destock). Industrial $52M (+32%), Medical record $71.3M FY25 (+39% in Q1'26) are the offsets. Consumer small. Auto concentration is both the secular bull (EV/safety content) and the cyclical risk.
⑤ 主要客戶
The #1 structural flag. Melexis ≈ 40–45% of revenue ($364M in 2024; down from 55% in 2018) — AND a related party (the same Duchâtelet/De Winter families control both X-FAB and Melexis). Related-party pricing is hard for minorities to verify, and ~half of revenue rides one affiliated buyer's auto cycle. (Unverified aggregator claims that NVIDIA/Broadcom are customers are NOT in filings — treat as false.)
⑥ 護城河
Real but narrow, stable-to-slightly-eroding. (a) Automotive/medical qualification lock-in (IATF 16949, AEC-Q100, ISO 26262/13485) → 12+ yr sticky design-ins. (b) One of very few open/merchant SiC+GaN foundries (most SiC is captive in IDMs). (c) Western/diversified footprint = a sourcing moat. Weakness: sub-scale; many mature-node analog competitors (Tower, VIS, GF).
⑦ 管理層展望
Cautious — and notably DECLINED FY2026 full-year guidance citing 'limited visibility' (a tell). Q2'26 guide: revenue $190–200M, EBITDA margin only 17–20%. Expects a 'steady recovery' of auto in H2'26 as Melexis destock ends. Tone = trough-with-hope, not boom.
⑧ 為何是卡點
Arms-dealer to EV/industrial electrification + early AI-power/AI-optics optionality. Every EV needs more analog/power/sensing silicon; SiC is the power chokepoint for traction inverters & (emerging) 800V AI-datacenter racks; silicon-photonics feeds AI transceivers. BUT the AI exposure is indirect, early and pre-scaled-revenue — the live driver remains auto cyclicality, mid-trough.

3 · A two-speed business — the bull legs are real but small

Approx. Q1'26 technology mix (shares overlap/est.). CMOS ~80% is in an auto-driven trough (the consensus recovery call). Microsystems & Photonics ~17% is at an all-time high and accelerating — the under-covered secular leg (silicon-photonics on the XPH90 platform, partners SMART Photonics / LIGENTEC, AI-optics-relevant but pre-scaled-revenue). Wide-bandgap SiC/GaN ~8% is re-accelerating off a crushed base (*+152% YoY but it FELL −34% in FY25 and is loss-leading into a glut). The mix is the whole debate: you're buying a cyclical CMOS foundry with two small, exciting, not-yet-material options on top.

5 · Reverse-model & scenarios — what €9.20 already assumes

At €9.20 (EV ~€1.45B, ~8× FY25 EBITDA) the market is pricing a successful re-rating to a "specialty AI-infra foundry" — a clean H2'26 auto V-recovery and margin re-expansion to the mid-20s and SiC turning from shrinking to growing and silicon-photonics converting to Tower-like AI-optical revenue (which it does not yet earn) and a structurally higher multiple than its own history. That is a double-count of margin recovery + multiple expansion. A defensible mid-cycle math: EBITDA ~€180M × 5–6× → EV ~€0.9–1.1B → ~€5.0–6.4/share.

⚠ The #1 risk — related-party customer concentration (governance).

Melexis is ~40–45% of revenue AND a related party: the same Duchâtelet / De Winter families control both X-FAB and Melexis (strategic family + Sarawak-state holders ~61–73%; free float ~27–39%, estimates vary by source). So roughly half of revenue depends on one affiliated buyer, related-party pricing is hard for minorities to verify, transfer-pricing and capital-allocation conflicts are structural, and minorities have little say. The thin float amplified the meme spike up — and will amplify any reversal down. This single fact caps the multiple a disciplined investor should pay.
