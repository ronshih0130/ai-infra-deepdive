EXPLAIN IT SIMPLY · 白話版

This dashboard answers four questions in order. (1) What's the weather? rates/growth/inflation set whether the tide lifts boats (now: strong growth, inflation at a 3-yr high but the oil spike that caused it is reversing, so the Fed can stay parked without getting more hawkish). (2) Is the tank near empty? — how greedy/leveraged the crowd is (margin debt is at a record and price-extension just hit a 52-week high; that's late-cycle fuel-gauge, not a timer). (3) Which of our warning lights actually work? — we backtest each indicator so we don't act on ones that merely feel predictive (margin-debt YoY barely leads; the yield curve and the component-shortage signal genuinely do). (4) Where is money trending vs snapping back? — at the sector level and, crucially, inside AI-infra: a momentum sub-segment you ride; a mean-reversion one you buy on dips, not chase.

Method, caveats & what to watch

方法與風險

How the regimes were computed

  • Data: 2-yr weekly closes (105 bars) for 32 instruments via IBKR (read-only). Baskets = equal-weight of constituent weekly returns.
  • Momentum vs MR: Lo-MacKinlay variance ratio (q=8), lag-1 autocorrelation, and a rescaled-range Hurst exponent; regime = majority vote.
  • Integrity: computed YTD cross-checked vs IBKR's independent snapshot. The four flagged sectors (SMH/XLE/XLY/XLC) differ by 1–7pp purely from a year-boundary bar effect — IBKR weekly bars are dated by week-start, so the last-2025 bar closes on Jan-2, shifting the YTD base. Trailing 13w/52w/regime metrics are unaffected; broker-snapshot YTD is used for the L1 heatmap.
  • Backtests: price-based tests are 2-yr weekly, n≈45–605 — directional, not statistically strong. Macro backtests cite longer-history published studies.

Forward triggers — the watch list

  • Regime flip: Networking-Si VR8 crossing <1 = ride is over; Optics/Substrates RS re-cross up = dip is bought.
  • Macro swing: does the oil pullback hold? WTI <$75 + CPI <4% revives H2-cut odds (bullish duration/AI); WTI >$95 re-arms the inflation shock. Watch the FOMC dot-plot Jun-17.
  • Overheat → action: curve re-inversion (FRED T10Y3M), HY OAS > ~350–400bp, or a breadth divergence. Margin debt / price-extension alone are not triggers.
  • Sentiment fat-pitch: F&G <20 + put/call >0.8 + VIX >25 + NAAIM <50 = real capitulation (we are not there — bears high but VIX/P&C complacent).
  • Emerging-trend tells: new lead-time blowouts (#1), capacity-exit (#6); capacity-add announcements (#7) are the top-warning. CPO pull-in (optics) & OSAT util <80% (substrates) are the fundamental top-warnings.
