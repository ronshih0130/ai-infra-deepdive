Highest-alpha contradictions

Per the framework, the best signals come from contradictions & bottleneck rotation (capex rising but cloud prices falling; backlog rising but margins falling).

  • The SAME names are CLOUD's 'cheap pocket' AND MARGIN's 'first domino': the neocloud de-rating (CRWV -28%, NBIS -36%, IREN -37% MoM) reads as a tightness-high/priced-low dislocation in CLOUD, but MARGIN flags these exact levered names (CoreWeave ~8.9x D/E, interest ~25% of revenue; Nebius pledging GPUs+contracts for a $775M loan) as the fragile edge that cracked first in mid-July. Do NOT treat the neocloud selloff as clean alpha — it is the highest-risk line in the deck; wait for late-July neocloud prints.
  • PKG is internally split by design: the headline binding sub-link (CoWoS 2.5D interposer) is EASING (gap 20%->10%, capacity to ~200k wpm) at the same moment ABF substrates + hybrid bonding are TIGHTENING — 'packaging is the chokepoint' is only half-true this week; be long the substrate/tool/metrology leg (4062.T/ATS.VI/BESI.AS/ONTO), NOT the interposer/CoWoS leg (TSM/AMKR, priced).
