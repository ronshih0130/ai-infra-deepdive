Highest-alpha contradictions

Per the framework, the best signals come from contradictions & bottleneck rotation (capex rising but cloud prices falling; backlog rising but margins falling).

  • ACCEL vs MEM on the memory/packaging cycle: ACCEL frames the first cracks appearing (Trainium2 supply>demand, CoWoS easing) suggesting the supply cycle is topping, while MEM insists HBM tightness is 'NOT rotating away — it is broadening' with no relief until 2028; the two nodes disagree on whether the upstream silicon/memory cycle is peaking or still extending.

  • CLOUD signal=neutral / priced=8 vs its own confirmed_shortage stage and tightness=9: the node calls physical demand 'still far exceeds deliverable capacity' yet rates the signal neutral and priced high because the Meta-Compute supply-side crack offsets the demand tightness — the aggregate-tightness and equity-signal are pulling in opposite directions.
  • MARGIN (signal=bear, MONETIZE lens) routes its best alpha to ETN/VRT/MSFT — the SAME power/cooling arms-dealers that GRID and COOL flag as already 'richly priced'; the FCF-squeeze logic says buy the arms-dealers while the supply-side nodes say those exact names are the priced-out leg, so ETN/VRT sit on both the rotating-in (via MARGIN) and rotating-out/priced (via GRID/COOL) side.
