Verification finding (read before acting) — the raw scan surfaces accelerating TICKERS, not vetted theses.

A name-level verification pass on the top picks found two ticker-identity errors and a purity problem (`top-bom-alpha-names-verification.md`): 6231 = Insyde (系微, BMC/UEFI firmware), NOT the TLVR-inductor maker "Cyntec" (which is 2452, delisted into Delta in 2010) — the accel signal is real but belongs to firmware, not power-inductors (real inductor exposure routes via the listed pure-play Tai-Tech 臺慶科 3357 or diluted parent Delta 2308 — though 3357 has itself already run, +117% YTD). 6442 = Insilight (光聖, AI-fiber), NOT "Sintai" — a real high-purity fiber name but already recognition/euphoria (~64× P/E), so the "un-run" tag was wrong. AcBel / Lite-On are heavily blended (true AI revenue ~8–20%), so their monthly tape is a noisy AI proxy. Lesson: this L6+awareness scan is a DISCOVERY/triage layer — every candidate needs an identity + AI-revenue-purity + valuation check before it's a position. 8 · Method & honest caveats — rerun monthly after MOPS posts (~10th)

Leading signal = Taiwan monthly operating revenue (FinMind, free, ~25mo history) → 3-mo-MA YoY acceleration; for US names, quarterly revenue YoY-acceleration (weaker, no monthly cadence — which is the thesis: TW monthly names are data-richer). Awareness = Yahoo 52-week price position + YTD. Quadrant: accel>0 & pos<0.85 = EARLY-ASYMMETRIC.

Caveats (do not over-trust): (1) Calibration done (`calibration.md`, PIT month-clustered backtest): the acceleration signal shows a positive but unproven cross-sectional lean (IC +0.079, positive in 15/19 months, but Newey-West t=1.54 — not significant after adjusting for overlapping windows). Pooled, momentum (PRICED) actually won this AI-bull regime, so the not-priced filter is a risk/drawdown overlay, not a return-maximizer. Suggestive triage, NOT proven alpha; parameters now locked for prospective out-of-sample reruns. (2) Several TW MOPS reads are blended legacy-heavy (Chenbro/GCE/Wus/ADATA/AcBel/LELON) — the monthly number is a noisy proxy for the AI line; verify AI mix share. (3) Acceleration is a fundamentals signal, not a price-timing signal — early names drew down before paying in the worked cases. (4) Korea/Japan/US names have NO monthly read (quarterly only) — their "lead" is weaker. (5) L3 distributor layer now WIRED (DigiKey + Mouser APIs) — adds the part-number lead-time cross-check; the L6×L3-CONFIRMED flag requires revenue accel AND lead-time blowout to agree. Known blind spot: allocated/bespoke AI parts are not in catalog so return non-stock. Bill-of-lading (ImportYeti) remains 403-blocked. (6) Extreme YoY (Credo +465%, Phison +301%, Micron +174%) are real AI inflections but sanity-check before sizing. Size small on the deduction; add on confirmation.
