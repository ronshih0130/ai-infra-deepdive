A1 · Plain-language: what is an OCS? 光路交換機是什麼？

An OCS is a telephone switchboard for light. A normal data-center switch (EPS, electrical packet switch) reads every packet: light → electrons → chip decides → back to light. That costs power (~3,000W-class for 136 ports) and locks you to one speed generation. An OCS never reads the data — it just steers the beam with tiny mirrors (or liquid-crystal phase arrays) from one fiber to another, like patching a cable, in ~milliseconds. ~108W, speed-agnostic, and it never needs upgrading when optics go 400G→800G→1.6TC04. 中文：OCS 不解讀封包，只是把光「反射」到另一條光纖 — 像自動化的配線盤。因為不碰電，功耗是電交換機的 ~1/30，而且升級到 1.6T 也不用換機器。

Why it matters now: Google proved it at scale (a decade, quietly, ~$3B claimed advantageC04) and in 2026, for the first time, outside merchants (Lumentum, Coherent) are shipping OCS in volume to Google + Oracle, with Meta/Microsoft/Amazon/NVIDIA samplingC03. The reflex trade is the box-makers. This run asks the deeper question: what scarce thing does every OCS — captive or merchant, mirror or liquid crystal — have to pass through?

A10 · Risk register & honest holes

Bear case / risks

  • Funded disintermediation: on-chip OCS backed by Alphabet + Microsoft venture arms (C10). If wafer-scale OCS works by 2028, the free-space collimator+mirror toll evaporates. (Mitigant: 6dB loss, 32×32 ports, H2-2027 earliest — and Run #16 taught us "2028 disintermediation" rarely kills a 2026-27 ramp.)
  • Google concentration: ~85-90% of 2026 units (est.). A captive-share shift or Google capex pause dominates every merchant model (C01).
  • Samples ≠ orders: the $4B TAM needs MSFT/META/AWS conversion — zero production orders exist outside Google/Oracle today (C03).
  • OCP standardization → multi-vendor interop → price competition (the Run #10 UQD pattern) (C11). A parallel Chinese OCS chain (Huawei CloudMatrix, est.) reinforces the commoditization tail (C15).
  • MEMS 3-year wear shifts share to LCoS/waveguide over time (C09) — bad for the mirror-die volume story specifically.
  • SILEX-specific: P/E ~50 on undisclosed OCS mix; IPO lockup expiries ahead (unverified dates); Sai 45% overhang; SEK; ~$10.7M/day liquidity.
  • Single-source economics: the entire BOM table (C12) and the ~70% Silex share (C06) trace to one specialist publication. If SemiFundamental is wrong, the component thesis needs rebuilding.
