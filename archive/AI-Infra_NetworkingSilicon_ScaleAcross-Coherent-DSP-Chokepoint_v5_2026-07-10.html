01

Executive snapshot

卡點 · the scarce link
EXPLAIN IT SIMPLY · 白話版

An AI "supercomputer" is thousands of GPUs wired together. There are three ways to wire them, by distance. Scale-up = GPUs in one rack, joined by copper (Run #17). Scale-out = racks across one building, joined by short optical fibre (Runs #5/#11). Scale-across = GPUs in different buildings, even different towns, because a single site cannot get enough electricity — this is the new problem of 2026. Wiring across towns means sending data down tens of kilometres of glass fibre, and that needs a special chip called a coherent DSP that can rebuild the signal after it has been smeared by the long journey. Very few companies can make one. That chip is the chokepoint. The catch: the two companies with the best coherent DSP (Ciena, Nokia) keep it for themselves and sell you the whole box, and their stocks have already doubled this year. So this is a good story that the market has mostly figured out — the cheap, early money is in the laser-and-modulator "engine" that sits underneath the DSP, which almost nobody buys yet.

THE CALL — one paragraph

Scale-across is the genuinely un-priced use-case of AI networking, and its binding silicon is the coherent DSP — every cross-building link passes through one. But three things cap the trade: (1) the best coherent DSP is captive (Ciena WaveLogic 6, Nokia ICE) — a repeat of Run #11's "the scarcest IP hides inside the system, not a merchant chip"; (2) the finalists have already run +95–182% YTD; and (3) for the biggest volume tier — campus DCI under ~10 km — a lighter "Coherent-Lite" (or even direct-detect) can substitute, so the DSP is not an unconditional monopoly. Net: this is own-the-integrated on a pullback (CIEN, or NOK as the cheaper diluted proxy), not fresh alpha. The early dollar is in the coherent optical engine — the InP tunable laser + modulator below the DSP — and in the diluted Acacia-in-Cisco and OCS watchlist.

own-it (pullback): CIEN lower-multiple proxy: NOK merchant DSP, priced: MRVL early = engine below DSP + OCS watchlist

Leans are personal opinion for a private notebook — not a recommendation or solicitation.

VARIANT — what the Street misses

Consensus frames "AI networking" as scale-up copper (NVLink; Amphenol/Credo) + scale-out Ethernet (Broadcom/Arista/NVIDIA). Broadcom's PAM4 DSP sits in nearly every 800G transceiver — but that is the scale-out chip, not the scale-across chokepoint. The un-priced third leg runs on coherent silicon.

The disaggregation edge: within scale-across, the scarce link is the coherent DSP, but the best one is captive and the merchant tier (Marvell/Acacia) wins volume at lower margin. And a live bifurcation — campus DCI can go "Coherent-Lite", thinning DSP content for the biggest pool. So "buy the coherent DSP" is directionally right but the rent settles at the integrated systems house, not a merchant chip.

WHY THE CELL MOVED HERE

A single AI site is capped by the local grid (~hundreds of MW to ~1 GW). To train one model on more compute, hyperscalers stitch multiple campuses into one logical cluster (SemiAnalysis: OpenAI/Google multi-DC; Google Ohio ≈ three campuses ≈ 1 GW). The moment a link leaves the building it needs coherent optics — and the coherent DSP is the one component with no easy second source. That is why the scarcity migrated out of the rack (copper) and the building (SerDes) into the cross-building tier this run opens.

03

Layer 3 — the coherent link, drawn

what's actually inside a scale-across link

A scale-across link is a chain of specialised parts. Below, each key sub-component drawn as a small picture, with its job and who makes it. The coherent DSP is the brain; the optical engine (laser + modulator) is the co-scarce muscle one layer below it.

Coherent DSP CHOKEPOINT

Encodes bits onto light's phase & amplitude, and rebuilds them after 2–1000s km of fibre smear (dispersion, noise). Requires leading-edge 3nm + decades of DSP/FEC IP.
Makers: Marvell (merchant, Inphi/ClariPhy), Cisco/Acacia (merchant), Ciena (WaveLogic, captive), Nokia (ICE, captive), Huawei

InP tunable laser co-scarce

The clean colour-tunable light source. Grown on indium phosphide — a hard, low-volume III-V material. "A TFLN modulator win still pulls an InP laser behind it": the durable choke below the DSP.
Makers: Coherent (6″ InP, Sherman TX), Nokia (captive InP fab, Sunnyvale), Lumentum

High-baud modulator TFLN rising

Imprints the DSP's electrical signal onto the light (phase + amplitude, I/Q). At 1.6T+ the field is shifting from InP Mach-Zehnder to thin-film lithium niobate (TFLN) for lower drive/heat.
Makers: Coherent, Lumentum, captive at Ciena/Nokia; HyperLight (private TFLN)

DCO module integration

"Digital coherent optics" — DSP + laser + modulator + receiver assembled and tested into a plug that drops straight into a router/switch (IP-over-DWDM), no separate transponder shelf.
Makers: Marvell, Cisco/Acacia, Ciena, Fabrinet (assembly), InnoLight/Eoptolink

DWDM line + router system

Combines dozens of coloured channels onto one fibre, amplifies and manages the span. Where the integrated houses capture full-stack margin — or get disintermediated if the plug goes straight into a router.
Makers: Ciena, Nokia, Cisco, Arista (routing)

Scale-across DCI demand

The buyer: hyperscalers stitching GW-scale campuses into one cluster. Meta is leading 800ZR+ IP-over-DWDM adoption. This pool grows ~27%/yr to 2028 (est., Nokia).
Buyers: Meta, Google, Microsoft, OpenAI/Oracle and neoclouds

Where each technology runs — by reach

deployment mapping

The same "connect two GPUs" job is served by different physics at different distances. This is the substitution map that both drives and caps the coherent-DSP thesis: coherent owns everything beyond ~2 km, but campus links (2–10 km) are the contested zone where "Coherent-Lite" — or even direct-detect — can thin the DSP.

Beyond ~2 km, direct-detect optics run out of link budget and coherent takes over — that is the structural demand for the coherent DSP. But the fat near-term volume (intra-metro campus, 2–10 km) sits in the dashed contested zone, where a reduced "Coherent-Lite" DSP or linear/LPO approaches can substitute — the single biggest reason severity is 16, not 18.

The chokepoint & severity — coherent DSP

4-factor /20

Coherent DSP — the scarce silicon that survives long glass

Every scale-across link — the wire between two buildings — passes through a coherent DSP: a leading-edge (3nm) chip encoding data onto light's phase/amplitude and rebuilding it after kilometres of dispersion and noise. At 400G it was an Acacia + Marvell duopoly; at 800ZR/1.6T the systems houses (Ciena, Nokia) are re-integrating captive DSP for the premium tier while Marvell holds a ~12-month merchant lead on 800ZR. It is a genuine chokepoint — but a contested one, and campus reach offers a substitution path — so it scores 16/20, below the rotation's true monopolies (GOES 18, glass cloth 19).

SEVERITY SCORECARD — coherent DSP link 16/20
TIGHTNESS ≠ A BUY

The link is tight, but remaining asymmetry is only ~6/20. Three reasons the trade is largely spent: (1) the finalists are +95–182% YTD; (2) the best DSP is captive — you can only buy it wrapped in a fully-valued systems house; (3) the merchant tier is a diluted slice of a mega-cap (Marvell's story is XPU, not coherent). Severity measures how hard the link is to replace; asymmetry measures what is left to earn. Here they diverge sharply.

VARIANT / DISAGGREGATION — the captive-DSP trap + the reach bifurcation

Consensus buys scale-up copper and scale-out Ethernet and treats "AI optics" as one euphoric blob. The disaggregation: (1) the scale-across chokepoint is a different chip — the coherent DSP, not Broadcom's PAM4; (2) the best coherent DSP is captive (Ciena WaveLogic, Nokia ICE), so the scarcest silicon has no clean merchant vehicle — a verbatim repeat of Run #11's SerDes finding; and (3) the biggest near-term volume — campus DCI under ~10 km — can run "Coherent-Lite," thinning DSP content exactly where the ports are. So the coherent DSP is real and binding, but the rent settles at the integrated systems house, and the merchant chip is both diluted and reach-limited.
