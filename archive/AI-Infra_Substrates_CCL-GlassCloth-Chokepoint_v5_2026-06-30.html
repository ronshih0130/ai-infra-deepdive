01

Executive snapshot

為什麼是現在

Every AI accelerator board — the GPU baseboard, the NVSwitch tray, the 1.6T network switch, the Rubin mid-plane — is a stack of copper-clad laminate (CCL): copper foil + resin pressed onto a woven glass-fibre cloth. When signals run at 224 Gbps per lane, an ordinary cloth leaks the signal away, so the board must use ultra-low-loss "T-glass" / low-Dk cloth — and that special weave is made almost entirely by one 108-year-old Japanese spinner, Nittobo (Nitto Boseki), at ~90% share. The cloth is only ~19% of a board's material cost but it is the link with no second source and the longest lead-time, so it is exercising real pricing power (two +20%-class hikes in a year). The catch for an early-alpha mandate: the secret is out — the whole laminate complex has already run +100% to +240% YTD. This report maps where the rent actually sits, scores the chokepoint, and grades each play early-vs-priced.

EXPLAIN IT SIMPLY · 白話版

Think of an AI circuit board as a layer cake. Each layer is a sheet of woven glass cloth soaked in plastic resin, with a sheet of copper foil on top — pressed together, that sandwich is "copper-clad laminate," and a GB300 AI board stacks 28 to 34 of them. When the chips talk at 224 billion bits a second, a normal glass cloth acts like a wet sponge and soaks up the signal, so the board needs a special ultra-clear "T-glass" weave. Here's the punchline: that special cloth is made almost entirely by one Japanese company, Nittobo — and you can't build a glass furnace overnight, so it has the whole AI industry over a barrel and is raising prices. It is a tiny part of the board's cost but the part nobody can replace — exactly like Ajinomoto's ABF film a few years ago. The investing question: who keeps the shortage-money, and after a year where these stocks doubled to tripled, is any of it still cheap?

Every laminate sits at one (board type × material layer) coordinate. Prior substrate runs covered the IC-substrate directly under the chip (Run #6 ABF film, Run #12 glass-core/TGV). This run is the PCB the package is soldered onto — the much larger board, woven from glass cloth, that every AI accelerator needs.

03

The four materials, illustrated

材料元件

A CCL sheet is four materials pressed together. Three are inputs (cloth ★, copper foil, resin); the fourth is the pressed result (the laminate). The glass cloth (★) is the scarce, hard-to-replicate link — it needs a glass furnace and a proprietary low-loss recipe that take years to build and qualify.

Glass cloth ★ CHOKEPOINT

The woven fibreglass skeleton of every layer. Low-Dk "T-glass" leaks far less signal at 224G. Needs a special melt recipe + furnace; yields are the binding constraint.
Nittobo ~90% T-glass · AGC, Taiwan Glass, Asahi Kasei ramping (small)

HVLP copper foil 2nd link

The conductor. At high frequency, current rides the surface, so the foil must be ultra-smooth (low-profile). The biggest single cost in a CCL — and harder to make at HVLP grade than commodity foil.
Mitsui Kinzoku (leader) · Furukawa, SK Nexilis, Nan Ya

Resin 3rd link

The plastic glue that fills the cloth and sets the dielectric properties. Low-loss PPE/hydrocarbon chemistries replace cheap epoxy at the high end — fewer formulators than it looks.
MGC, AGC, SABIC, Shin-Etsu (PPE/hydrocarbon)

CCL sheet value gate

The finished sandwich the PCB fab buys. The maker who co-formulates cloth+foil+resin and passes a 12–18-month NVIDIA qualification captures the integration rent.
EMC #1 (16%) · TUC, Shengyi, Kingboard, Doosan, Panasonic

Cost share (copper 42% > resin 26% > glass 19%) is the inverse of scarcity rank (glass cloth > copper foil > resin). The cheapest input is the binding one — because only one firm can make it at grade.

05

The grade ladder — which cloth for which chip

材料分級

Each NVIDIA platform steps the laminate grade down in loss (Df) and up in price. The switch boards lead the ladder (hardest signal-integrity bar); the cloth gets scarcer and the qualified-maker count shrinks at every rung.

As the grade climbs, the qualified-supplier count collapses — and at every rung the glass cloth underneath is the same Nittobo-dominated material. The Taiwan CCL duo (EMC + TUC) AI-grade share is modelled to rise ~42% (2024) → ~85% (2027) [Goldman, single-broker]; M9 reportedly has <5 qualified producers globally. Note: the "M-grade" ladder is an informal industry convention (derived from Panasonic's Megtron naming) used loosely in broker notes; the Df thresholds shown are approximate (est.), not a single standardized spec.

08

The chokepoint & severity

卡點評分

Low-Dk glass cloth — the cloth one firm weaves and no one can replace fast

Glass cloth is ~19% of a CCL's cost yet the single hardest link to source: Nittobo makes ~90% of T-glass, the low-loss weave that 224G boards require, on furnaces that take years to build and ~6 months to reach stable yield. With demand exploding through the M8→M9 step-up and capacity relief not until mid-2027, Nittobo is exercising the textbook chokepoint privilege — sequential +20% (Aug-2025) and +20–30% (Apr-2026) price hikes that cascade into BT (~1Q lag) and ABF (~2Q lag) substrate quotes. This is the same pattern as Ajinomoto's ABF film: a tiny-dollar, near-monopoly input that gates a trillion-dollar buildout.

REMAINING-ASYMMETRY GAUGE

Composite remaining asymmetry ≈ 6/20. The whole complex ran +100–240% YTD; the chokepoint owner (Nittobo) is the least-financialized of the priced (thinnest coverage, biggest pullback off the high) — recognition, not latent. Genuine latent dollars are in §9's watchlist.

VARIANT / DISAGGREGATION — and how it could be wrong

Consensus buys the board makers (the CCL stocks) because that is the visible AI-PCB trade. The disaggregation: the board makers compete (≥6 qualified CCL firms, NVIDIA deliberately multi-sources — Doosan just passed the GB300 compute tray EMC reportedly failed), so the durable, un-substitutable rent sits one layer above the board — at the glass cloth (Nittobo ~90%) and, more latently, the weaving/lamination equipment (booked through Q1-2028). How it's wrong: (1) Nittobo is itself tripling capacity and tolling ~20% to Nan Ya by 2027 — a monopolist that floods its own moat caps the rent (the severity-erosion case). (2) The reported figures flatter both ends — Nittobo's headline net is inflated by a ~¥34B one-off; EMC's "AI margin explosion" is really ASP/price-hikes (gross margin only +~2pp/yr). (3) Long-run, co-packaged optics removes copper trace area and could shrink the highest-layer boards — a 2028+ disintermediation, not a 2026 one. (4) The real second-source debate is whether AGC / Taiwan Glass / Asahi Kasei reach production grade before the shortage breaks — if they do by 2027, the ~90% share is the high-water mark.
