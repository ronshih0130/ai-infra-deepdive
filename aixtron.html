THE CENTRAL RECONCILIATION · key takeaway

Bulls and bears AGREE on 2028 revenue (~€700–950M). The most-cited bear (mwb, SELL/PT €30) models 2028 rev €800.7M / EBIT €208.2M; consensus is ~€906M; the bull is ~€1.0B. That is a narrow band — the debate is NOT about revenue. The real debate is whether a proven violent cyclical keeps a ~35× compounder multiple FOREVER. At €56 you are paying for "yes, forever." The normal-WACC DCF above shows that the moment the multiple de-rates to a cyclical terminal (~13–16× EBIT), fair value is €20–30 — even on the bull revenue path.

This reconciles §2 with the §5 scenarios explicitly: the §5 scenario values (bear €30, base €44.5, bull €72) all implicitly assume the premium multiple HOLDS in proportion to how good the world gets — the bull €72 is the bull revenue path at a ~33× held peer multiple; the bear €30 is essentially the same revenue path re-rated to a cyclical terminal. So §5 is the multiple-regime distribution, and §2's reverse-DCF is the "if-it-de-rates" anchor underneath it. The asymmetry comes from the fact that the multiple has far more room to fall than to rise.
