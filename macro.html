EXPLAIN IT SIMPLY · 白話版

This dashboard answers four questions in order. (1) What's the weather? rates/growth/inflation set whether the tide lifts boats (now: strong growth, but an oil spike pushed inflation to a 3-yr high, so the Fed stays parked). (2) Is the tank near empty? — how greedy/leveraged the crowd is (margin debt is at a record; that's late-cycle fuel-gauge, not a timer). (3) Which of our warning lights actually work? — we backtest each indicator so we don't act on ones that merely feel predictive (margin-debt YoY, it turns out, barely leads; the yield curve and the component-shortage signal genuinely do). (4) Where is money trending vs snapping back? — at the sector level and, crucially, inside AI-infra: a momentum sub-segment you ride; a mean-reversion one you buy on dips, not chase.

Because every segment's fundamentals are still accelerating (§5, §7-research), a price pullback in those names is far more likely valuation/positioning-driven (a dip to buy) than the start of a fundamental top. The genuine top-warnings to watch are the fundamental ones: a CPO timeline pull-in threatening pluggable optics, and OSAT mainstream utilization slipping below ~80% — neither is flashing yet.

Monday refresh protocol — turning weekly-close levels into an open-bell decision

The execution levels are weekly-close (06-08). At Monday open, for each name pull the live price and apply this state machine against the §-above 20w/40w MA levels:

  • BUY NOW — live price is within ~3% of the add-zone (20w MA) AND RS still > its 20w avg AND not gapping up >5% premarket.
  • ALERT ONLY — price is 3–10% above the add-zone: set an alert at the 20w MA, don't initiate.
  • NO CHASE — price is >10% above add-zone OR at/within 1% of 52w high with RS-z > 2 (the crowding-guardrail names): wait for a reset.
  • TRIM / STOP — weekly close below the 40w MA (structural) or below the 20w MA for momentum names: cut.
  • GAP RULE — if a name gaps >5% on news premarket, stand down for the first 30–60 min; let the open print resolve before acting.

Today most ride-names (ALAB, MRVL, MU, AMAT, KLAC, COHR, AMKR) are in NO-CHASE (at highs); the BUY-NOW/accumulate candidates near their add-zones are AVGO, ETN, GEV, VRT, FN.

Mechanical risk-down rule — the hedge trigger

Cut gross / add an index hedge only when ≥2 of 3 fire on a weekly close: (1) HY OAS >350bp for 2 consecutive weeks; (2) 10Y-3M re-inverts (<0); (3) SPX makes a 3-month high while the NYSE A/D line OR %>200DMA fails to confirm (breadth divergence). Reset / re-risk when all three normalize for 2 weeks. This keeps you long the record-leverage tape until the backtested-to-lead signals — not margin debt alone — actually turn.

Response ladder: 1 of 3 fires = no action, raise alert. 2 of 3 = trim gross ~20% and/or add a 1/3-notional QQQ/SPY hedge; cut the extended crowding-guardrail names first. 3 of 3 = trim gross ~40% and/or hedge 2/3-notional. Reset: unwind hedges when all three normalize on weekly close for 2 weeks.

De-risking waterfall (what to cut, in order): 1st — at-highs / crowded (MU, MRVL, ALAB, CRDO, AMAT, KLAC, COHR, AMKR): most downside if the tape turns. 2nd — high-beta momentum (VRT, LITE, GEV, MOD): trim to the core. 3rd — core compounders (AVGO, TSM, NVDA, ETN): cut last, only on 3-of-3. Keep the latent watchlist (§5) dry powder — a forced de-risk is when the still-latent names get their entry.

Method, caveats & what to watch

方法與風險

How the regimes were computed

  • Data: 2-yr weekly closes (104 bars) for 32 instruments via IBKR (read-only). Baskets = equal-weight of constituent weekly returns.
  • Momentum vs MR: Lo-MacKinlay variance ratio (q=8), lag-1 autocorrelation, and a rescaled-range Hurst exponent; regime = majority vote.
  • Integrity: computed YTD cross-checked vs IBKR's independent snapshot field; benchmarks & most L1 matched within tolerance (high-vol AI names differ by a 6-day timing gap, not error).
  • Backtests: price-based tests are 2-yr weekly, n≈50–600 — directional, not statistically strong. Macro backtests cite longer-history published studies.

Forward triggers — the watch list

  • Regime flip: Networking-Si VR8 crossing <1 = ride is over; Optics/Substrates RS re-cross up = dip is bought.
  • Overheat → action: curve re-inversion (FRED T10Y3M), HY OAS > ~350–400bp, or a breadth divergence (index highs while A/D / %>200DMA roll). Margin debt alone is not a trigger.
  • Sentiment fat-pitch: F&G <20 + put/call >0.8 + VIX >25 + NAAIM <50 = real capitulation (we are not there).
  • Emerging-trend tells: new lead-time blowouts (#1), capacity-exit (#6); capacity-add announcements (#7) are the top-warning. CPO pull-in (optics) & OSAT util <80% (substrates) are the fundamental top-warnings.
