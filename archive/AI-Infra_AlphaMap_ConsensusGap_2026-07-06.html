6-term glossary
alpha
Edge from knowing something the market hasn't priced yet — getting in EARLY, before consensus re-rates the stock. Not the same as a good company.
consensus-gap
The distance between your differentiated bottom-up estimate and what the Street/price already assumes. A big, un-priced gap is where money is made.
financialization filter
Hard rule: a name with >15 analysts + deep options is EARLY-ineligible — the print hits every desk the same second, so it can only be CONFIRMING or PRICED, never fresh alpha.
priced-check (reverse-model veto)
Before calling anything EARLY, back out what the price already implies. If your view doesn't beat the price-implied expectation by more than the error band, the edge is gone — veto it.
EARLY vs PRICED
EARLY = gap open, revision barely started, asymmetry intact. PRICED = the move and the up-revision are already in the stock; chasing it is paying for a beat that's already discounted.
the two modes
CONSENSUS-GAP (a real Street number exists; be early to a forced revision) vs DISCOVERY (under-covered, no clean consensus; anchor on the price-implied expectation + a dated catalyst).
