Highest-alpha contradictions

Per the framework, the best signals come from contradictions & bottleneck rotation (capex rising but cloud prices falling; backlog rising but margins falling).

  • ACCEL: physical accelerator supply is still ~3:1 demand-bound (real tightness 8) yet the EQUITIES are priced 9 and just had their worst session since 2020 — tightness and tradable alpha have decoupled; own the adjacency (ALAB) or the laggard (AMD), not the priced leaders.
  • GRID is the stated binding node (tight 9) but POWL/ETN/GEV have run hard — the cleanest possible asymmetry is the laggard sub-links (Quanta EPC/labor, Siemens Energy Grid Tech on Xetra), not the consensus US equipment names.
  • PKG: the headline CoWoS chokepoint is actually EASING (gap 20%->10%) while the real 2027 scarcity (ABF film, hybrid bonding) sits one layer up — node tightness 9 is carried by the substrate/bonding leaf, not the CoWoS leaf everyone watches.
  • COOL: node still labelled confirmed_shortage and priced 8, but tightness has begun to ease (7->6) with ~40 CDU vendors signalling a coming shakeout — the durable thesis is now upstream POWER, with COOL the relative-safety/decelerating leg.
  • MARGIN: demand is unambiguously bull across the stack, yet aggregate hyperscaler FCF crosses to ~zero in Q3'26 and ORCL already runs -$23.7B FCF on $134.6B debt — the not-yet-consensus-validated risk is financial-quality/leverage, not demand.

Method & honest caveats

Two data layers. (1) Computed (free, deterministic, reruns weekly): SEC EDGAR XBRL revenue-acceleration + gross-margin trend + inventory/RPO (point-in-time via filing date), Yahoo 52-week position + relative-strength vs ^SOX, live DigiKey + Mouser part-number lead-times for the catalog-distributable nodes (power silicon now up to 66.0wk, MLCC/passives ~24wk, retimers ~26wk, memory modules ~30wk), public cloud GPU rental pricing from Lambda/RunPod/Azure, LBNL interconnection queue requested/IA-executed MW for system-level GRID stress, Texas TDLR/TABS data-center project registrations plus a derived utility follow-up watchlist for site-level construction evidence and next MW/service-date proof targets, a source-linked transformer supply rowset, and a source-linked ISO/utility large-load rowset for direct data-center/load evidence. The estimates scaffold validates consensus_estimates.csv, FMP prior earnings events, FMP earnings-calendar timing rows, and FMP analyst-awareness rows, but current-snapshot / timing / recognition-stage rows do not unlock the PIT action gate. Nodes with no free exact feed yet (HBM contract price, CoWoS/ABF capacity, CPU attach/availability) carry research-sourced leading metrics instead, flagged as such. (2) Research (web, sourced, per subsector + SemiAnalysis article/ChipBook-derived metadata): an independent agent verifies tickers, assigns the shortage stage, and pulls recent sourced evidence. SemiAnalysis rows use article titles/URLs and derived classifications; if subscription body text is used, it is transient and not saved. This layer is refreshed weekly and adversarially self-reviewed by Codex (gpt-5.5, highest effort) role-playing a retail investor.

Caveats — do not over-trust. (1) The "priced-in" axis is a price-position proxy, not a valuation model; with ^SOX +101%/6mo the whole sector is extended, so COMPUTED-INFLECTION names are relatively un-run, not cheap. (2) US quarterly fundamentals lag a monthly channel-check; the live edge is the distributor lead-time + the research layer, not the 10-Q. (3) Curated stages carry a confidence flag — low-confidence nodes are hypotheses. (4) Taiwan-listed names (Tai-Tech, GUC, Alchip, Quanta, Yageo, Unimicron) are the true pure-plays in several nodes but are not in your IBKR access — they are shown for completeness and routed to a tradable proxy; the proxy is an imperfect substitute. (5) This is a discovery/triage map, NOT a buy list — every candidate needs an identity + AI-revenue-purity + valuation check before sizing. (6) IBKR is READ-ONLY; nothing here is investment advice.
