COILED-SPRING SCREEN · 蓄勢待發多鏡頭篩選 · RESEARCH CONTEXT

Weak tape, loaded spring: a multi-lens screen for stocks basing out with an unpriced forward catalyst

A screen of 1,604 liquid US names (mcap ≥ $500M) for stocks whose price narrative is negative or flat — but whose latest earnings call implies significant revenue recognition or large orders inside the next two quarters, whose insiders are not selling, and whose tape shows a base forming. Built bottom-up from FMP data; every catalyst claim is quote-grounded to a transcript and adversarially cross-examined by independent skeptic agents, then clustered into segment trend narratives where several independent calls point at one upstream driver.
01

Executive snapshot

執行摘要

Explain it simply · 白話版

A coiled spring is a stock everyone has stopped talking about — it fell or drifted sideways, the sellers exhausted themselves, and the chart went quiet. But inside the company, management is already telling you (on the earnings call, in plain words) that big revenue or orders land in the next one or two quarters — and the market hasn't moved on it yet. Insiders quietly not selling (or better, buying) is the tell that the people with the best information agree. This dashboard hunts for that exact overlap: quiet chart × loud transcript × silent insiders. The point is to be EARLY — before the crowd re-rates the story.

Coil / base · 打底52-week price well off its high, but volatility contracting and lows rising — sellers done, spring compressing.
Forward catalyst · 前瞻催化Management statements on the call implying large revenue recognition or orders within the next two quarters.
Priced-in · 已反應If the stock already outperformed the S&P by >8% since the call, the market heard it — the spring already released.
Insider signal · 內部人訊號Open-market buys are bullish; routine stock grants are noise; heavy CEO/CFO selling kills the thesis.
Under-coverage · 認知落差How few people are telling this story — thin news flow and few analyst actions mean the re-rating is still ahead.
Adversarial verify · 對抗性驗證Two independent skeptic agents attack each top name: "is it already priced?" and "is the catalyst real?"
02

The framework — five scored lenses, two skeptics, one composite

多鏡頭評分框架

MECE by information source: the tape (what price/volume says), the transcript (what management says), the insiders (what informed money does), the estimates (what the sell-side embeds), and the coverage (who is telling the story). Each lens scores 0–100; the composite weights them with the forward catalyst heaviest. Names that already ran take a −10 "reacted" penalty. The top 18 then face two adversarial skeptics before earning a tier — and in the ranked table, refuted names are demoted below every unrefuted name regardless of raw composite. (The −30 identity-mismatch penalty did not trigger this run: 60/60 names passed the ticker↔issuer check.)

Segment trend narratives — the same driver, told by many mouths

板塊趨勢敘事

One CFO promising a big quarter is a company story; three independent management teams pointing at the same upstream driver — a defense budget line, a weather cycle, a capacity shortage — is a segment story, and it is the strongest form of early evidence because each transcript is an independent evidence family. Segments below are clustered by demand driver, not GICS sector, then stress-tested by sweeping peer transcripts from the wider 315-name stage-1 pool for corroboration. Ranked by segment alpha score: corroboration breadth × catalyst intensity × group-level coil × under-coverage.

Verified candidates — the springs worth watching

Be precise about what WATCH means here: no name in this run survived both skeptics with full confirmation (0 PRIME). WATCH = the thesis was not refuted, but the skeptics still weakened it — most cards below carry double-WEAKEN verdicts, shown verbatim so you can judge the doubt yourself. Trigger board — what wakes each spring, and where to read it

Verification signals, not instructions: the concrete event that would convert the transcript promise into printed revenue, an estimated date, and where an amateur can verify it free (transcript, 10-Q, 8-K, press release). A story with no readable checkpoint is not a thesis. Nothing here is a trading trigger — it is a reading list.
